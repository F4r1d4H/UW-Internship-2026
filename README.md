# UW-Internship-2026
> This repository is where I will store information, code images, challenges etc. from my project at my UW internship. It is meant to act as a living journal of the project so that I can share it with people in the future!

**_Problem Statement/Context_**
 
The City of Waterloo Museum is hosting a family-friendly community event to inspire the next generation of innovators, and we would love for your group to be a part of it!
 
In honour of Women’s History Month, this event celebrates the incredible contributions women have made to STEM. It runs alongside the exhibition Iron Willed: Women in STEM. Our goal for the STEM Starts Here: Celebrating Waterloo Region Innovators, is to engage kids and families through exciting, interactive experiences.

**_Event Details:_**
Saturday, October 17, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Drop-in style) at Conestoga Mall – winners court
 
**_How you can participate:_**
We are inviting local groups to set up a booth and host a hands-on STEM learning activity for children and families. Whether it is a quick science experiment, a coding game, a building challenge, or a robotics demonstration, we want to give kids the chance to tinker, build, and discover.
 
We will provide the space within the mall; you just need to bring your passion and your activity!
If your group is interested in inspiring local youth and showcasing your work to the community, please let us know by July 30, 2026.

**_The Project_**
Design, Create and Code a turtlebot that can navigate through a maze. The turtlebot will be controlled by pre-set hand signals and will include basic obstacle avoidance. Additionally, I must design a user friendly interface that can be aesthetic yet simple and functional. Furthermore, I will code a Nao robot with basic conversational abilities so it is interacting with the user more, though this relies on more basic coding skills.
